## Professional Background & Motivation

I am a technical marketing engineer specializing in semiconductor products such as microcontrollers and memory solutions.

I graduated in 2018 from a French engineering school with a master’s degree in engineering. My background is rooted in embedded systems development, with several years of professional experience in application engineering. This hands-on technical foundation allows me to confidently support customers, translate complex technical concepts into clear explanations, and act as a bridge between engineering teams and market-facing roles.

What particularly motivates me is having a system-level view of how products come to life — from development and validation to promotion, customer integration, and long-term production support. Working at the intersection of engineering, marketing, and sales has given me a strong appreciation for how semiconductor companies bring products to market and how these technologies are ultimately used in real-world systems. In recent years, this perspective has led me to explore new domains of long-term impact.

I am also deeply interested in education and knowledge transfer. I worked as a private tutor in mathematics and physics for four years, an experience that reinforced my belief that teaching and explaining are core professional skills. The ability to explain complex topics simply not only helps others learn, but also sharpens one’s own expertise — a perspective that strongly aligns with the role of technical marketing.

Beyond my technical background, I have a strong interest in language learning and international collaboration. As a self-taught language learner, I have reached professional working proficiency in English, Korean, and Japanese, all of which I have used in a professional context. I have worked in France, Denmark, South Korea, and Japan, and also studied in Norway as an exchange student. These experiences have shaped my adaptability, cross-cultural communication skills, and global perspective on technology and collaboration.

## Professional Interests & Long-Term Focus

As my career has progressed, I have become increasingly interested in technical domains where system-level thinking, reliability, and long-term impact are critical. Beyond my current specialization in semiconductor products and technical marketing, I am intentionally deepening my understanding of the following areas, where technology plays a central role in shaping future infrastructure and society.

- **Artificial Intelligence and AI Safety**  
    I am particularly interested in the design, deployment, and safe operation of AI systems, especially where they interact with complex software and hardware stacks. Topics such as robustness, interpretability, and safety in real-world deployments resonate strongly with my background in embedded systems and my focus on explaining complex systems clearly and responsibly.

- **Sustainability-Focused Energy Systems**  
    I am drawn to energy technologies that prioritize efficiency, resilience, and long-term sustainability. From power electronics and embedded control to large-scale infrastructure and system integration, I am interested in how engineering decisions can reduce environmental impact while maintaining reliability and scalability.

- **Security, Cryptography, and Cybersecurity**  
    Security is a foundational requirement for modern systems, particularly as connected devices and digital infrastructure continue to expand. I am interested in topics ranging from applied cryptography and secure embedded design to broader cybersecurity challenges, with a focus on building systems that can be trusted and maintained over time.


These areas reflect the direction in which I want to continue learning and contributing professionally. They align with my interest in foundational technologies, critical infrastructure, and responsible system design, and they provide a long-term framework for how I evaluate opportunities and challenges in my career.

## Values & Guiding Principles

I care deeply about how technology, infrastructure, and institutions shape our societies over the long term. The following principles guide how I think about my work, the systems I contribute to, and the world I want to help build:

- **Sustainable and human-centered environments**
I am strongly interested in urban and infrastructure models that prioritize public transportation, walkability, and quality of life, while reducing pollution, noise, and environmental impact.

- **Responsible use of technology and media**
I am concerned about the effects of modern digital platforms on attention, focus, and critical thinking, and I value technologies that empower people rather than exploit their time or behavior.

- **Long-term thinking over short-term incentives**
I believe that economic and organizational systems should prioritize long-term stability, resilience, and societal benefit rather than short-term financial gains.

- **Public interest in critical infrastructure**
Sectors such as energy, transportation, and security play a fundamental role in society and require strong governance, transparency, and accountability to serve the common good.

- **Environmental responsibility and moderation**
I value sustainability, reduced over-consumption, and durable, high-quality products over disposable and resource-intensive alternatives.

- **Education, discipline, and collective responsibility**
I believe education and social cohesion rely on clear expectations, mutual respect, and a balance between individual freedom and collective well-being.

- **Fairness, rule of law, and social cohesion**
A functioning society requires shared rules that apply to everyone, as well as institutions capable of enforcing them fairly and consistently.

- **Purpose-driven organizations**
I am drawn to organizations that aim to create value responsibly, whether non-profit or for-profit, without pursuing growth at the expense of people or the planet.
